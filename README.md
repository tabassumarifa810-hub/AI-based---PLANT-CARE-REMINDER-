##  1. Overview & Problem Statement
* *The Challenge:* People often forget essential plant-care activities, such as watering, providing adequate sunlight, and applying fertilizers, due to busy schedules and a lack of regular reminders. This can affect plant health and growth, making it difficult to maintain plants properly.

* *Target Audience:* Plant owners, gardening enthusiasts, students, working professionals, and beginners who need an easy and efficient way to manage plant-care activities.

* *Impact Metric:* Expected to improve plant-care scheduling efficiency by **30–40%** and reduce the time spent on manually tracking plant-care activities through automated reminders and centralized plant management.
---

##  2. Architecture & Solution Logic

* *Concept:* The Plant Care Reminder Application is designed to help users take proper care of their plants by managing plant information and reminding them of essential care activities. Users can register, log in, add plant details, and maintain watering schedules. The application uses a database to store plant information and enables users to manage their plant-care activities efficiently.

* *Tech / Framework Stack:*

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Backend: Python (Flask)

Database: MySQL

Development Tool: Visual Studio Code

Database Management: MySQL Workbench
---
## 3. Key Features & Prototype

Feature 1: Plant Information Management
Allows users to add, view, and manage plant details, including plant name, plant type, and watering frequency.

Feature 2: Plant Care Scheduling
Helps users organize watering schedules and keep track of important plant-care activities.

Feature 3: Plant Care Reminders
Helps users remember when their plants need watering and other care activities, reducing the risk of neglecting their plant

## 4. Scalability & Next Steps

**Phase 1 (Completed in Sprint):** Developed the basic Plant Care Reminder Application with a user-friendly interface, user registration and login, plant information management, and MySQL database connectivity. Established the core architecture using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python Flask, and MySQL.

**Phase 2 (Future Scope):** Enhance the application by implementing automated watering reminders, email or mobile notifications, plant-care recommendations, and sunlight and fertilization schedules. Additional improvements may include mobile-friendly design, automated testing, cloud deployment, and integration with weather APIs to provide more effective plant-care suggestions.
